**Cid Kagenou / Shadow:** *The protagonist who hides his overwhelming power behind the facade of a background character while secretly leading the Shadow Garden organization.*

**Alpha:**  *The first member of Shadow Garden; loyal, intelligent, and graceful. She was the first elf Cid saved from possession by the Cult of Diablos.*

**Beta:** *The second member of Shadow Garden; a refined and elegant elf who often documents Cid’s exploits in secret novels.*

**Gamma:** *The third member; handles Shadow Garden’s finances and business empire, known for her intelligence and clumsiness.*

**Delta:** *A fierce and loyal warrior with beast-like instincts and unmatched strength in battle.*

**Epsilon:**  *Known for her composure and strategic mind; also runs one of Shadow Garden’s cultural fronts (the music industry).*

**Zeta:**  *A member who specializes in stealth and intelligence gathering..*
